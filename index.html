Interactive Gestures & Controls
Drag mouse or touch inside phone viewport:
  • Swipe up to unlock: Lock Screen
  • Swipe down from middle: Pull up App Searcher
  • Swipe down from top corners: Open Notif or Control Center
  • Press Home Button twice during incoming call: Hang up call
  • Swipe left/right on notification: Dismiss notification
  • Swipe up card inside App Switcher: Removes card without closing switcher.
